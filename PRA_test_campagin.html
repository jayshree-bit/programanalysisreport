Observations & Recommendations

What the Campaign Data Tells Us

Observations
  • Campaign duration spanned 36 business days.
  • Prospects were engaged through telephonic calls and promotional emails.
  • Two assets were promoted throughout the campaign.
  • ‘How North American Banks Modernize Their Core’ was the most frequently viewed asset, with 65.0% of its views coming from the United States.
  • Financial Services contributed 56.7% of total engagement.
  • Companies with 1001–5000 employees showed 71.7% engagement.
Recommendations
Prioritize the strongest-performing asset and the Financial Services audience in future outreach.
Prioritize Ohio, New York, Arizona, Illinois and Tennessee, which collectively account for 58.3% of engagement.
Use the 1001–5000 employee segment as a priority audience for follow-up and future campaigns.
